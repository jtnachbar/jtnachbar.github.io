About

Hi, I'm Jamie. I'm most at home between theory and application — proving something about scheduling or routing, then shipping the code that makes it real. My interests run through robotics, automation, and artificial intelligence, but really any problem that yields to math and code.

I care about rigor and about things that run. The projects I'm proudest of started as a question about a model and ended as software someone could actually use.
